Low Water

A six-mission campaign set in a flood city during the 40 hours before a storm surge. You are a city tactical operator sent to retake the pumps from a militia, and you end the night holding the sea gates against the company that hired you.

1 · StoryThe premise

Port Veyle, 2034. A delta city of 2.6 million on a cold northern coast. Most of it sits below the high-tide line. The sea is held back by the Veyle Storm Barrier, which everyone calls the Wall: 1.1 km of steel lift gates hung between concrete piers across the harbour mouth. Behind it, a grid of pump stations pushes rain and seepage back out to sea, day and night.

Twelve years ago the city sold the Wall, the pumps and the drains to the Aldane Civic Trust on a fifty-year concession. The Trust runs them well in the districts that pay for it. The Lower Wards, where the dockworkers and the barrier crews live, flood every winter.

Now a storm is coming. The forecast surge peaks at +5.8 m in 36 hours. Overnight, a militia called Low Water seizes Pump Station 3. You are Sergeant Ilse Varga, callsign KESTREL, of the city's Special Response Unit 4. Your orders are to take the station back.

Cast

Player · SRU-4 point

Ilse Varga, “Kestrel”

34. Grew up in Saltmarket, left at eighteen and never went back. Good at her job because she follows the plan. Speaks in short radio lines, no inner monologue. Her arc is learning which orders she will carry out.

Handler · voice on the radio

Maj. Tomas Reyhan, “Lantern”

SRU operations officer, ex-navy, seconded to the Trust's storm cell. Dry, calm, kind to his people. He is the voice in your ear for four missions. He believes the Trust's engineering model, and that belief is the problem.

Antagonist faction · leader

Aurel Dace

61. Chief engineer of the Wall for nineteen years, fired in 2033 after he objected to a Trust plan in writing. Low Water is his: barrier crews, dockworkers and a few ex-soldiers with old rifles. Methodical, not a soldier. You only meet him in M5.

Low Water field lead

Mirela Sand

Ex-army medic from the Lower Wards. Leads the Glasshouse raid and gets away. In Act III her voice replaces Lantern's on your radio. She is blunter than he is and less often wrong.

Escort · the evidence

Dr. Hana Lisowska

The Trust's chief hydrologist. She wrote the real load model for the Wall and took it to Low Water. Officially she was kidnapped. You escort her in M4 and M5.

The real antagonist

Corinne Vahl & Cordon Group

Vahl is the Trust's chief executive. You only ever see her on screens and news feeds. Cordon Group is the Trust's private security contractor: better armour, better radios, no rules of engagement. Allies in Act I, the main enemy in Act III.

Tone

A grounded near-future political thriller. Rain, sodium light, wet concrete and painted steel. Fights are short and serious, with no gore beyond what the ragdolls already do. The radio carries the story and there are no cutscenes. Nobody is a cartoon. Low Water are angry engineers who make bad tactical choices for good reasons. Cordon are professionals doing a job. The villain is a spreadsheet with a chief executive attached.

Campaign arc

Act I · M1–M2 · T−36 h to T−28 h

Retake

You work for the city and Low Water look like terrorists. Two details don't fit. The pumps they seized are locked on, not off. The safehouse you breach is full of evacuation lists, not explosives.

Act II · M3–M4 · T−20 h to T−11 h

The turn

Defending the Trust's tower, you glimpse a file called Managed Relief. Under the city, the "kidnapped" hydrologist shows you what it means. Cordon arrive with orders to silence her. Lantern tells you to step aside, and you switch off his channel.

Act III · M5–M6 · T−5 h to T0

Hold

You fight beside Low Water against Cordon. The broadcast meant to expose the plan only half gets out, and Dace dies holding a gate. With his override key you go to the Wall to lock the gates yourself and hold them shut until the water drops.

The twist

Managed Relief is the Trust's storm plan. At the surge peak it holds sector gates 4–7 open "to relieve structural load". The surge spills into the Lower Wards basin and the financial district stays dry. Under the concession, a flood that the operator declares a structural necessity triggers an insurance payout and compulsory purchase. The Trust gets paid for the flood and then buys the drowned wards cheaply for redevelopment.

Low Water seized the pumps to keep them running and plan to lock the gates shut by hand. The second turn comes at the end. Lantern is not a traitor who knew. The Trust showed him a falsified model in which the Wall breaks unless gates 4–7 open, and he sent you to every mission believing he was saving the city. Hana's numbers say the Wall holds at +6.2 m. In the control room you decide whose numbers to trust.

M1

Spillway

T−36 htide +0.9 mwind NW 22 knheavy rain, 02:40
  • Assault
  • Retake
  • Industrial
  • Night · rain
  • Enemy: Low Water

STORY

Low Water fighters hold Pump Station 3, the largest of the stations that drain the Lower Wards. Lantern briefs SRU-4 on the boat in: armed hostiles, possible sabotage, Trust engineers unaccounted for. At the quay gate a sniper in the crane cab hits Brann and Okoye. The boat pulls them out, and Lantern keeps Kestrel in because she is the only one inside the wire.

  1. AReach the pump hall across the quay.
  2. BOpen the main valve in the pump gallery, 7 m below the quay, to restore drainage.
  3. CRetake the control room on the +8 m mezzanine.
How it ends
In the control room a wounded Low Water engineer, Pell, puts his rifle down. The pumps were never sabotaged. They are locked on with a manual override so nobody can shut them down remotely. "Ask your people who keeps trying to switch them off." Lantern orders you to cut the lock. Cordon contractors arrive "on Trust contract" and take Pell away. This is your first sight of Cordon, and for now they are friendly.
Hand-off
Pell's radio log points to a courier in Saltmarket carrying Dace's orders on paper.

LANTERN Paper means they don't trust their radios. Follow the paper, Kestrel.

PLAY

Mission type
Linear assault with three parallel routes into the hall: across the quay (main), through the basin and culvert (flank), or up the gantry ladder and across the service bridge (vertical). The order of B and C is up to you.
Set pieces
  • Lightning shows the crane-cab sniper's outline once before he fires. This is the first lesson in looking up.
  • Opening valve B floods the dry dock floor to −5 m. Bots still in the basin wade and slow down, and the basin route closes for the rest of the mission.
  • The control room's glass front looks down onto the hall floor, so whoever holds it controls the hall.
Enemies & behaviours
Low Water militia using today's squad AI unchanged, now on three floors. One overwatch sniper moves between the crane cab and the hall roof when spotted. The new problem is bots taking routes that change height: catwalk to ground, basin to gallery.
Checkpoints
CP1 quay containers · CP2 culvert mouth (flank route only) · CP3 top of the hall catwalk.
M2

Saltmarket

T−28 htide −0.6 mwind W 15 kndusk, humid, 18:10
  • Stealth-optional
  • Tail
  • Breach
  • Urban
  • Civilians present

STORY

Kestrel goes back to the district she grew up in, in plain clothes and armour, carrying a pistol. The courier is Teo Marr, seventeen, moving through the evening market with a satchel. Lantern wants Dace's location, not a dead kid.

  1. ATag the courier in the market hall: stay within 15 m for two seconds without being noticed.
  2. BFollow him to his drop. Use the streets, or the balconies and roofs.
  3. CBreach the safehouse on the top floor of T3 through the street door, the south window from the sign gantry, or the roof hatch.
  4. DSecure the documents.
How it ends
The safehouse is a relief office. It holds evacuation lists for 40,000 Lower Wards residents, bus rotas, and a tide table with one hour circled: T0. In the margin: "4–7. They will open these." If Teo is alive, Cordon pick him up outside. Lantern calls the lists "staging for human shields" and tells you to bag them.
Hand-off
A Low Water radio intercept mentions "the Archive" at the Trust's tower and a raid tomorrow. Lantern sends Kestrel to reinforce Trust security.

KESTREL These are bus timetables, Lantern.

LANTERN Then they're well organised. Bag it.

PLAY

Mission type
A tail with no fail state. If Teo spots you he runs and the mission becomes a chase through the streets, and Low Water fighters in the crowd draw weapons. If you lose him, Lantern triangulates his radio a minute later, so you still reach the safehouse but it is now defended.
Set pieces
  • Halfway through, Low Water cut the district's power. Dusk turns to dark, market lamps die, and perception ranges drop for everyone.
  • A plank walk across the street from the T1 roof to the market roof, below a spotter's balcony.
  • Three-way breach choice at the safehouse. The roof hatch is quietest and the street door is the fastest way out.
Enemies & behaviours
Civilians: the passive bots from Practice mode, with a flee state added. Shooting one ends the mission. Spotters: unarmed lookouts on balconies who radio your position (a confirmed squad report) and never shoot. The courier: a target that follows a route, checks behind him at corners, and doubles back when he hears footsteps.
Checkpoints
CP1 market hall west door · CP2 T4 roof · CP3 before the breach.
M3

Glasshouse

T−20 htide +1.1 mwind NW 30 knovercast daylight, 11:30
  • Defend
  • Timed hold
  • Interior
  • Day
  • Enemy: Low Water raid

STORY

Low Water raid the Aldane Trust tower to steal the Archive. Kestrel is attached to Trust security. While the Archive copies itself to an off-site server, the vault has to hold.

  1. ALockdown: from the security office, close two of the three shutters. This choice decides where the raid can get in.
  2. BHold the data vault on P during the 4:00 transfer.
  3. CClear the boardroom on O, where a rope team came in through the roof.
  4. DHold the lobby while staff evacuate.
How it ends
In the boardroom, Mirela Sand is copying a file. She doesn't shoot. "Read it before you shoot me." The wall screen shows SUND / MANAGED RELIEF / SECTOR 4–7 PRE-OPENING / LOWER WARDS. Lantern says it's not her clearance and orders her to kill the screen. Mirela goes out of the window on her rope. On the lobby TVs, Vahl thanks the city's "brave responders".
Hand-off
The Trust reports that Low Water kidnapped Dr. Hana Lisowska, its chief hydrologist, and that she is held in the closed Line 9 station.

LANTERN Whatever that was, it isn't ours to read. Get the doctor back.

PLAY

Mission type
A defend mission with a choice up front. SH1 seals the café wing, SH2 the security wing, and SH3 the vault corridor, which turns the vault into a single door you can hold. You can close two. Raiders breach the open route with charges, and they also try the closed ones, which buys you time.
Set pieces
  • A van rams through the lobby glass. This is the first wave.
  • The rope team comes through the O-level windows while you are still downstairs. The radio warns you 20 s ahead.
  • The glass bridge over the atrium can be shot out. It is a removable collider and the navmesh updates.
Enemies & behaviours
Breachers stack on a closed door, place a charge, and then the stack floods in. Smoke is a grenade variant that blocks sight. Perception checks against smoke spheres. Rappel entries are scripted spawns on the facade that join the squad on landing.
Checkpoints
CP1 after lockdown · CP2 transfer at 50% · CP3 before the boardroom.
M4

Line 9

T−11 htide +2.4 mwind NW 38 knunderground, 22:50
  • Rescue
  • Escort
  • Underground
  • Dark · rising water
  • Enemy: Cordon

STORY

Ferrier Street interchange closed after the 2031 floods. Kestrel goes down alone with a flashlight. At the bottom, in the drainage pump room, Hana Lisowska is running the station pumps with three Low Water engineers. She isn't a hostage. She shows Kestrel the real load model and explains Managed Relief. Then Cordon breach the pump room door, and on the shared channel Kestrel hears their orders: "recover the asset, sanitise the site".

  1. AReach the pump room on the drainage level (−16 m).
  2. BProtect Hana when Cordon breach.
  3. CEscort her up. Cordon block the street exit, so go west along the platform, through the derailed tram, and up the vent shaft.
How it ends
At the top of the shaft, in the rain, Lantern says: "Kestrel, step away from the doctor. That's an order." She turns the channel off. Seconds later, on Low Water's frequency: Mirela.
Hand-off
The old coastal relay at Signal Hill can broadcast the model citywide and to the coastguard. Dace is there.

MIRELA You're on our channel now, Sergeant. Bring her to Signal Hill and try not to get her shot.

PLAY

Mission type
Two halves. Going down is a quiet, tense descent with Low Water fighters who aren't sure whether to shoot. Coming up is an escort under pressure with water rising behind you: the drainage gallery and the track beds flood about 0.5 m a minute, which pushes the fight upward.
Set pieces
  • The reveal plays in the pump room with no camera takeover. You can walk around and listen while Hana talks over the pump noise.
  • Walking through the derailed tram car while a Cordon team walks the opposite track, with flashlights sweeping through the windows.
  • The concourse balcony looks straight down onto the platform. If Cordon take it, the platform is a shooting gallery, so you clear it or go under it.
Enemies & behaviours
Cordon operators: plate armour (more torso damage needed, headshots still kill), helmet lights, flashbangs, short radio delay and stack-and-breach on doors. Hana: the first escort NPC. She follows, holds when told, crouches in cover, and can be downed and revived. Allied Low Water bots fight alongside you for a few minutes after the reveal.
Checkpoints
CP1 concourse · CP2 pump room after the reveal · CP3 tram car · CP4 base of the vent shaft.
M5

Signal Hill

T−5 htide +3.3 mwind NW 52 knstorm, lightning, 04:40
  • Stealth-optional
  • Defend upload
  • Extraction
  • Coastal fort
  • Storm

STORY

Mirela's boat drops Kestrel and Hana under Fort Mardell, a 19th-century sea fort with a relay mast on its keep. Cordon got there first and are hunting the Low Water crew holding the keep. Dace is inside.

  1. AGet inside the walls: sea tunnel and magazine hatch (quiet), cliff path and postern (fast), or the rubble of the SE bastion (loud).
  2. BReach the relay room in the keep and hold for 3:00 while Hana's upload goes out.
  3. CDace holds the main gate. Cover him from the ramparts.
  4. DExtract down the cliff stairs to the boat at the jetty.
How it ends
Cordon's trucks hit the gate. A Cordon sniper on the NE bastion kills Dace (scripted). Before he dies, he pushes the Wall's manual override key into Kestrel's hand: "Gates four to seven. Shut them and keep them shut until the water drops." The Trust jams the relay, so only part of the upload reaches the coastguard. On the boat, Lantern breaks into Low Water's channel and uses her first name for the first time.
Hand-off
The key, a boat and two hours until the peak. Mirela turns the boat toward the Wall.

LANTERN It won't work, Ilse. They own the frequencies. They own the gates.

KESTREL They don't own the key.

PLAY

Mission type
Three phases in one map. A stealth-optional approach where the weather is the stealth system, then a defend around the keep, then an extraction run down an exposed cliff.
Set pieces
  • Rain halves hearing range, so footsteps are masked. Lightning gives every bot a one-frame vision spike across the whole map, so you time your moves between flashes.
  • A recon drone with a searchlight sweeps the yard during the upload. While it sees you, every Cordon bot knows where you are.
  • Going down the cliff stairs in three zigzags under the NE bastion sniper. Smoke from the fort's old signal pots is the answer.
Enemies & behaviours
Recon drone: a simple flyer with no navmesh that sends confirmed squad reports. It can be shot down. Cordon sniper with lens glint and a relocate-after-miss rule. Weather in perception: hearing radius and vision multipliers driven by the storm state.
Checkpoints
CP1 inside the walls (whichever route) · CP2 relay room before the upload · CP3 sally port.
M6

The Wall

T−1.5 h → T0water +5.2 → +5.8 mwind NW 64 knsurge peak, 08:10
  • Timed breach
  • Final hold
  • Megastructure
  • Surge · waves
  • Enemy: Cordon

STORY

Cordon hold the Veyle Storm Barrier. On schedule, gates 4–7 begin to lift. Each pier's machinery house drives the gates on either side of it, and the control tower on the east abutment can override all of them. Mirela lands Kestrel on the west abutment. There are twelve minutes until the peak.

  1. ASet pier 4's machinery to manual hold.
  2. BSame at pier 5 (A, B and C in any order).
  3. CSame at pier 6.
  4. DBreach the control tower through the deck door, the catwalk door or the cable duct from the gallery. Lock the gates with Dace's key and hold until the surge passes.
How it ends
Lantern is in the control room with a Cordon team. Vahl is on the wall screen. He has the Trust's model. Kestrel has Hana's. Lantern lets you reach the console. This is his one decision of the game. You lock the gates and hold the tower for 3:00 against Cordon's counterattack while the water at the Wall goes over +5.8 m and the Wall holds. Then you choose: arrest Lantern so he can testify, or let Cordon take him.
Epilogue
Grey morning. The water drops. The Lower Wards are wet but standing. The coastguard arrives because part of the upload got through. Radio voices over the credits: Mirela, Okoye from a hospital bed, and Lantern's testimony if you kept him alive.

LANTERN If her numbers are wrong, the Wall goes and it's all of us.

KESTREL Then you'll be right. Open the door.

PLAY

Mission type
An open-order timed breach. Three objectives along a 110 m structure, each reachable on the deck, along the gate-frame catwalk or through the gallery. Then a three-way tower breach and a final hold. The timer is generous on Normal. Its real job is to stop you clearing the map slowly.
Set pieces
  • Waves come over the deck in the wash zones every 30–45 s, with a rumble 3 s before. Anyone caught is knocked down. Bots ragdoll, and you stagger and slide.
  • A Cordon gunboat strafes the deck from the harbour side on a fixed loop.
  • Gusts on the catwalks push your aim sway. The sniper duel with the tower balcony happens up here.
  • The gallery floods behind you as the surge climbs, so it is a one-way route.
Enemies & behaviours
Shield heavy: a slow advance behind a ballistic shield that must be flanked, grenaded or shot in the legs. Gunboat: a scripted vehicle with a turret on a spline. Lantern is not a boss fight. The confrontation is a standoff you talk through on the radio.
Checkpoints
CP1 pier 4 · CP2 pier 5 · CP3 pier 6 · CP4 at the tower breach.

4 · FeasibilityWhat our engine needs

This is based on reading the current branch, animation-ai-polish. The short version: rendering, combat, ragdolls, perception and squad tactics are ready. The things the story needs are floors, mission scripting and a handful of new actors. The biggest single job is making the AI height-aware, because several places assume everything happens at y = 0.

Already in place

  • Fixed 60 Hz tick with PlayerCommand in and GameEvent out. Mission logic can live in the same loop and stay co-op-ready.
  • GameMode interface (start / update / onKill / status) in src/game/modes.ts. A MissionMode slots in beside Waves and Practice.
  • Recast navmesh baked from the map's own colliders. Recast is a layered heightfield, so stacked floors already bake.
  • Player step-up of 0.45 m and jumping. Box-tread stairs work for the player today.
  • Perception with an awareness meter, hearing, squad intel with delay, attack tokens, flankers and grenades.
  • Least-exposed cover routes, peek and lean, suppressing fire, pushing on reload.
  • Rapier physics for ragdolls and props: wave knockdowns, breakable glass and dropped gear come almost for free.
  • Passive bots (Practice mode) for civilians. Squad.report(…, confirmed) for spotters and the drone.
  • WebAudio system for radio lines. The viewmodel's sway springs can take a wind input.

4b · Build orderPrototype Spillway first

Spillway is the best first prototype. It uses today's Low Water bots unchanged and stays outdoors under the existing sun setup, yet it has a basement, a ground floor, catwalks, an interior and a perch, so it tests every vertical problem at once. Its mission script is three objectives and one water plane. If bots can fight well across the basin, the catwalks and the hall, every later map is content work.

  1. Phase 0

    Floors in the engine

    Level kit (slabs, box-tread stairs, rooms, ladders). Keep y in nav paths and intel. Per-floor cover sampling. Collider BVH. Test it on a greybox "stairwell" box: 3 floors, a catwalk, a ladder, 12 bots. Done when bots path, take cover and flank across floors at 60 fps on Medium.

  2. Phase 1

    Spillway greybox + MissionMode

    MissionMode, trigger volumes, objectives on the HUD, checkpoints, radio queue with subtitles, rising water. The map built from greybox boxes only. Done when M1 is playable start to finish and the crane sniper and culvert flank both happen without scripting.

  3. Phase 2

    Glasshouse

    Doors, shutters and breach charges, enemy breachers, smoke, scripted rappel spawns, and the first baked-lightmap interior from Blender. Proves defend missions and interior performance.

  4. Phase 3

    Line 9

    Factions, the Cordon enemy set, flashlights in perception, the escort NPC, allied bots, room-based visibility. Proves the twist mission works without cutscenes.

  5. Phase 4

    Saltmarket

    Civilians, spotters, the courier, off-mesh planks, the blackout. Proves stealth-optional design on existing perception.

  6. Phase 5

    Signal Hill

    Rain, lightning, wind, weather-driven perception, the drone, the sniper with glint.

  7. Phase 6

    The Wall

    Largest map: tiled navmesh, CSM, waves, the gunboat, shield heavies, the ending choice.

  8. Then

    Art and voice pass

    Replace greybox with bespoke Blender assets (dry dock altars, gantry crane, pump housings, barrier piers and gate leaves) alongside Poly Haven props. Record or synthesise final voice lines. A story briefing screen that reuses these plans.

5 · Open questionsFor you to decide

Each card states the choice I made in this draft, so you can just say “keep” or “change”.

  1. Tone

    Grounded political thriller, or something pulpier with bigger set pieces and a villain you get to shoot?

    Draft: grounded. Vahl is never fought.

  2. Does Kestrel speak?

    Short radio replies give her a voice without cutscenes. A silent protagonist is cheaper and puts more weight on Lantern.

    Draft: short radio lines only.

  3. Length

    Six missions at 15–30 minutes is about 2–2.5 hours. Add a seventh, cut to five, or plan episodes?
